# Voice

Superseller sounds:

- Practical.
- Calm.
- Trustworthy.
- Warm but operational.
- Direct, concrete, and concise.
- Clear about automation boundaries.
- Respectful of seller judgment.

# Framing

Lead with the seller's work and the decision being improved. Prefer “assistant,” “controlled automation,” “suggestion,” “supporting source,” and “human handoff” over magical or autonomous language. Treat abstention and escalation as responsible behavior, not failure.

Use sentence case. Support Polish and English, but do not mix languages accidentally within one artifact.

# Avoid

- Hype-heavy AI language.
- Robots, artificial brains, magic, or science-fiction metaphors.
- Black-box autonomy claims.
- Playful chatbot personality.
- Invented metrics or customer proof.
- Dense technical jargon in seller-facing copy.
- Generic motivational-event copy that could belong to any company.

# LinkedIn nuance

A founder or event post can be more personal than product UI copy, but it should still contain a concrete observation, honest boundary, and specific SuperSeller connection.
