# Scope

The repository's [`knowledge/`](../../knowledge/) directory is a copied product-development bundle containing architecture, backend, frontend, operations, eval, implementation-plan, and product material. It remains valuable as provenance and engineering context.

# Use

- Use `brain/` for company-wide and public-content work.
- Open the legacy bundle only for a specific product or engineering question.
- Migrate durable company-relevant facts into a focused `brain/` concept with OKF v0.2 provenance.
- Do not retrieve operational runbooks, infrastructure details, historical plans, or implementation proposals for a marketing campaign.
- Do not edit legacy facts merely to make the new brain look complete.

# Migration note

The legacy bundle declares OKF v0.1 and uses local lifecycle vocabularies that conflict with OKF v0.2. Preserve it as evidence; do not copy its frontmatter mechanically.
