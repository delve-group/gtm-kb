# Editorial territories

These are working content pillars derived from current product truth. They are not yet human-approved official pillar names.

1. **Controlled automation** — sellers choose the mode; humans retain the exceptions.
2. **Answers grounded in real shop knowledge** — catalogue, offer information, shop policies, and rules inform replies.
3. **Confidence, evidence, and responsible escalation** — missing support becomes a handoff rather than an invented answer.
4. **Practical Allegro operations** — repetitive product, delivery, return, invoice, and policy questions.
5. **Test before trust** — simulator, evidence, confidence, and decision history make behavior inspectable.
6. **Learning from seller judgment** — meaningful edits can become reusable preferences.
7. **Before and after purchase** — buyer support and post-purchase communication belong to one operational workflow.

# Selection rule

A campaign should lead with one pillar and use at most one supporting pillar. The event or current company goal determines the angle; do not squeeze every feature into one post.
