# Campaign principle

Turn one real company moment into one clear story. The post should feel observed and specific, not manufactured from a feature list.

# Required campaign package

1. Research brief with live sources and dates.
2. Recommended angle and two alternatives.
3. A primary post plus concise variant.
4. Keyword and topic rationale.
5. Ranked tagging suggestions with URLs, evidence, confidence, and spam risk.
6. Graphic brief or generated graphic tied to the visual system.
7. Evidence receipt and review checklist.

# Post composition

- Open with the concrete event, tension, or learning.
- Connect it to one content pillar.
- Explain what SuperSeller is building in plain language.
- Use only supported claims.
- End with one relevant CTA.
- Prefer one memorable idea over a catalogue of features.
- Keep hype, generic inspiration, and algorithm folklore out of the draft.

# Keyword research

Research current language at execution time. Distinguish search terms, natural phrases, and hashtags. Recommend only words that fit the post's actual subject and Superseller's current goal. Do not present estimated popularity without a source.

# Tagging

- Prioritize confirmed organizers, venue or partner organizations, explicitly involved people, and consenting team members.
- A public LinkedIn profile is not proof of event involvement or permission.
- Mark uncertain identity or involvement and omit it from the default post.
- Prefer a small relevant set over broad attention-seeking tags.

# Approval

The generated post is always review-required even when every source concept is approved. Never publish, message, follow, or tag anyone automatically.
