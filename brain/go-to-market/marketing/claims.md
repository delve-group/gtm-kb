# Source-backed candidate claims

These claims are supported by the current product bundle but remain review-required in the new Company Brain until a human approves this concept:

- Superseller uses the seller's Allegro catalogue and shop knowledge.
- The seller can choose automatic replies or editable suggestions.
- Sellers can test answers and escalations before activation.
- Unsupported or uncertain cases can be passed to a person.
- Decision details can expose sources, rules, confidence, and outcomes.
- Meaningful seller edits can become reusable preferences.
- Shop-wide and offer-specific knowledge can work together.

# Confirm immediately before publication

- Official homepage, signup, waitlist, or demo URL.
- Prices, allowances, discounts, and extra packs.
- Exact time or cost savings.
- Customer counts, testimonials, logos, adoption, or performance metrics.
- Enterprise availability, dedicated support, or service levels.
- Email, SMS, push, or native app-store availability.
- Multiple Allegro account support.
- Referral rewards.
- Data residency, uptime, certifications, or compliance claims.

# Prohibited guarantees

Never state or imply:

- Guaranteed GDPR or EU AI Act compliance.
- Impossible hallucinations.
- Prevention of all data leakage.
- Guaranteed reply speed, automation rate, or time savings.
- Human-free handling of every buyer conversation.

# Rule

No source means omit the factual claim or request confirmation. A plausible sentence is not evidence.
