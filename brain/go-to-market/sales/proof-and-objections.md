# Proof backlog

Collect and verify:

- Median first-response time.
- Messages handled automatically.
- Human escalation rate.
- Suggestion acceptance and edit rate.
- Weekly operator time saved.
- Catalogue knowledge coverage.
- Knowledge gaps discovered through escalations.
- Post-purchase delivery success.
- Approved customer quotes, logos, and before/after screenshots.

Until this evidence exists, prefer product demonstrations and screenshots over performance claims.

# Objection guidance

| Concern | Grounded response direction | Do not claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “AI may invent answers.” | Superseller grounds replies in shop and offer knowledge, exposes evidence and confidence, and escalates unsupported cases. | That hallucinations are impossible. |
| “I need control.” | The seller chooses automatic replies or editable suggestions, defines rules, and can test behavior before activation. | That no supervision is ever needed. |
| “Our products are different.” | Offer-specific and shop-wide knowledge can be used together. | Universal compatibility without discovery. |
| “Complaints are risky.” | Complaint-related and explicitly human cases are escalation candidates. | Legal or compliance guarantees. |
| “Will this save time?” | Demonstrate the repetitive workflow being reduced. | An exact time-saving number without measured proof. |
