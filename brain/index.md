# Superseller Company Brain

This is the canonical, company-wide knowledge bundle for Superseller. Use progressive disclosure: open this index, then only the smallest relevant area.

## Company knowledge

* [Company](company/) - identity, story, durable facts, and company-level knowledge gaps.
* [People](people/) - team roles, expertise, public profiles, and tagging or media consent.
* [Product](product/) - externally useful product truth, capabilities, and vocabulary.
* [Strategy](strategy/) - current goal, positioning, strategic choices, and non-goals.
* [Go to market](go-to-market/) - audiences, sales knowledge, claims, content, and channel playbooks.
* [Events](events/) - dated event briefs, participants, sources, media, and outcomes.
* [Research](research/) - dated external research and source-handling protocol.

## Enabling knowledge

* [Operations](operations/) - company-brain collaboration and knowledge cadences.
* [Engineering](engineering/) - curated pointers into product and engineering reference knowledge.
* [Governance](governance/) - schema, publication safety, maintenance, and review rules.
* [Assets](assets/) - brand voice, visual system, and canonical asset inventory.

## Reading order for a public campaign

1. [Publication policy](governance/publication-policy.md)
2. [Current goal](strategy/current-goal.md)
3. [Product overview](product/overview.md) and [public claims](go-to-market/marketing/claims.md)
4. [Brand voice](assets/brand-voice.md) and [visual system](assets/visual-system.md)
5. The relevant [current event](events/vibe-coding-summer-jam-session-02.md) and [team directory](people/team-directory.md)
6. The smallest relevant sales or marketing playbook

Public generation may use only supported facts and must surface review-required claims.
