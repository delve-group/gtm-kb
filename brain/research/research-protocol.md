# Research at campaign time

- Research all time-sensitive event, person, company, keyword, platform, and market facts when the campaign is created.
- Prefer official event, company, product, and person-controlled sources.
- Use independent primary sources when a material claim benefits from corroboration.
- Record the source URL, page title, publication or event date when available, and access date.
- Separate observed facts from inference.
- Never invent reach, popularity, search volume, event attendance, or profile ownership.

# Identity resolution

- Match people using more than a shared name: role, organization, location, event relationship, or a person-controlled source.
- Assign `high`, `medium`, or `low` confidence with a short reason.
- Low-confidence identities are omitted from the default post and tag set.
- Public availability does not imply permission to tag, quote, or reuse a photo.

# Keyword research

Distinguish:

- **Topic:** the substantive subject the post addresses.
- **Natural phrase:** wording readers or buyers may recognize.
- **Search term:** a query supported by research evidence.
- **Hashtag:** an optional discovery label, not a substitute for clear prose.

Do not make platform-algorithm claims without a current primary source.

# Promotion into the brain

Research output begins as an artifact. Promote only durable, reusable findings with a source, owner, lifecycle, freshness date, and explicit publication state. Search results and generated summaries are not automatically company truth.
