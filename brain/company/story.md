# Confirmed founding context

Superseller originated within Delve Engine, a four-person product-building collective. Michał Sarzała founded and initiated the collective; Rafał Karwot, Igor Stalmach, and Patryk Rasiak are its founding members.

All four are co-founders of Superseller and continue to build it through Delve Engine:

- Michał Sarzała is co-founder and CEO.
- Rafał Karwot is a co-founder and backend engineer focused primarily on the AI-agent side.
- Igor Stalmach is a co-founder and frontend engineer responsible for visual direction, interface design, and frontend implementation.
- Patryk Rasiak is a co-founder and backend engineer responsible for billing and payment infrastructure.

Superseller remains a product built and owned through the Delve Engine collective while developing as a business venture in its own right.
