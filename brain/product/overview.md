# Product summary

Superseller is an AI customer-support assistant for Allegro sellers. It handles buyer conversations using the seller's approved shop knowledge, product information, and response rules. A seller can allow automatic replies or require approval of AI suggestions. Unsupported, sensitive, low-confidence, complaint-related, or explicitly human cases are passed to an operator.

# Core promise

Automate repetitive buyer support without giving up control over information, rules, sending mode, or risky cases.

# Positioning

For Allegro sellers who spend too much time answering repeat questions, Superseller is a controlled AI assistant that uses their actual catalogue and shop policies. Unlike a generic chatbot, it follows explicit seller rules, shows why it made a decision, and escalates when it lacks reliable support.

# Customer problem

Allegro sellers repeatedly answer questions about product specifications, compatibility, size, availability, delivery, order status, discounts, returns, complaints, invoices, and shop policies. Manual handling consumes time and produces slow or inconsistent replies. Unrestricted AI creates a different risk: invented facts, unsupported promises, mishandled complaints, and exposed personal information.

Superseller is designed to reduce repetitive work while treating refusal and human escalation as normal, responsible outcomes.
