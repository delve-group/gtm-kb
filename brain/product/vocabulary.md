# Prefer

- Superseller
- AI customer-support assistant
- Controlled automation
- Seller rules
- Shop knowledge
- Product or offer knowledge
- Automatic reply
- Editable suggestion
- Human handoff or escalation
- Evidence, supporting source, confidence, and decision history
- Allegro seller

# Avoid

- SuperSeller or previous product names
- Generic chatbot
- AI magic
- Fully autonomous support
- Human replacement
- Hallucination-proof
- Perfectly safe
- Guaranteed compliance
- Unlimited AI, unless a current approved commercial concept explicitly supports it

# Style

Explain concrete seller workflows and boundaries. Use operational language rather than futuristic metaphors.
