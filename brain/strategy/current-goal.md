# Active goal

Build a team-wide Superseller Company Brain that agents can consume and update, then demonstrate its usefulness through a one-prompt LinkedIn Campaign Studio.

# Demo outcome

Given a short request about the current event, the studio should create:

1. A grounded LinkedIn post in an appropriate Superseller voice.
2. Fresh keyword and topic research tied to the current goal.
3. A ranked list of relevant people and company accounts to consider tagging.
4. A graphic that follows the Superseller visual system.
5. An evidence receipt showing brain concepts, external sources, confidence, and unresolved claims.
6. A reviewable proposal for any durable knowledge learned during the campaign.

# Success principle

The demonstration should make the value obvious to founders: useful work appears from one normal prompt, company truth stays inspectable, and the brain improves without silently rewriting itself.
