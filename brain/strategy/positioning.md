# Differentiators

- Allegro-specific rather than generic omnichannel support.
- AI-first rather than ticketing-first.
- Seller-controlled rather than autonomous at any cost.
- Evidence-oriented rather than black-box.
- Product knowledge generated from active Allegro offers.
- General shop knowledge and offer-specific knowledge used together.
- Seller rules applied before generated wording.
- Choice between automatic sending and suggestion approval.
- Meaningful seller edits can become reusable learned rules.
- Simulator for testing before activation.
- Explicit human escalation as a normal product outcome.
- Inspectable decision and usage history.
- Pre-purchase support and post-purchase communication in one product.

# Anti-positioning

Superseller is not a generic chatbot, “AI magic,” autonomy without seller control, or a traditional helpdesk overloaded with ticketing clutter.

Never claim perfect safety, impossible hallucinations, complete data-leak prevention, or guaranteed legal compliance. The strategic story is responsible control and evidence, not infallibility.
