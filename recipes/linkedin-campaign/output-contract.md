# LinkedIn campaign output contract

Every campaign directory contains these files:

## `campaign.md`

- Campaign objective.
- Recommended angle and short rationale.
- Primary LinkedIn post.
- Concise alternative.
- Suggested CTA.
- Review checklist.

## `research.md`

- Research timestamp and search scope.
- Confirmed current facts with direct source URLs.
- Inferences and unresolved conflicts.
- Keyword table: phrase, kind, relevance, evidence, and recommended use.
- Research limitations.

## `tags.md`

| Candidate | Profile or page URL | Relationship | Identity confidence | Involvement confidence | Consent | Spam risk | Recommendation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Explain every non-obvious recommendation. Low-confidence identity or involvement means omit by default.

## `graphic-brief.md`

- Message hierarchy.
- Required dimensions and safe margins.
- Exact visible copy.
- Palette and typography.
- Logo or text-lockup behavior.
- Imagery direction and prohibited treatments.
- Alt text.

If a graphic is generated, store it beside the brief as `graphic.png` or `graphic.jpg` and visually inspect it before handoff.

## `evidence.md`

| Claim or element | Source | Source kind | Publication state | Confidence | Action required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Include the brain concept paths, external URLs, stale or review-required state, and any unsupported wording removed from the draft.

## `writeback-proposal.md`

- Durable facts worth proposing to the brain.
- Suggested destination concept.
- Evidence and owner.
- Facts that must remain campaign-only.

This file is a proposal, not an automatic mutation.
