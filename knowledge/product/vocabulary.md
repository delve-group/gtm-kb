## 25. Product vocabulary

- **Buyer** — Allegro customer who sends a message.
- **Seller/operator** — user managing the connected Allegro account.
- **Automatic reply** — AI-generated response sent without seller approval.
- **Suggestion** — AI draft awaiting approval, editing, or dismissal.
- **Escalation** — conversation intentionally handed to a person.
- **General knowledge** — shop-wide information used across products.
- **Product knowledge** — information scoped to an Allegro offer.
- **Manual rule** — explicit instruction written by the seller.
- **Learned rule** — reusable preference inferred from a seller's edit.
- **Intent** — classified purpose of the buyer's message.
- **Route** — handling path: rules, knowledge, or human.
- **Confidence** — score estimating whether retrieved knowledge supports an answer.
- **Conversation segment** — current logical exchange within a longer Allegro thread.
- **Priority post-purchase message** — offer-specific template that overrides the general message when matched.
- **Reply allowance** — number of AI replies available in the billing period.
